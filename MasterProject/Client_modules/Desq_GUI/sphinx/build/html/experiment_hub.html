Experiment Hub¶

Experiments are at the core of this platform - and running static, live, multi-figure, complex grid-form experiments are all compatible.

This section explains how to structure and write an experiment on your Desq. It covers creating the experiment file, implementing methods, setting up configurations, and executing experiments.
